# Campaign Stakes / Historical Crisis

**Status:** EXPLORING

UltraSim's free-form persistent simulation may benefit from a major narrative stake that gives a new character/world a clear reason to act without turning the game into a short linear campaign.

Current preferred shape:

> **A major historical event is approaching. The player can influence it, ignore it, fail it, or survive its aftermath. It is not the end of the simulation.**

Possible framing: a Convergence/Reckoning/Long Night-style crisis 12–24 in-game months away.

The value borrowed from a "stop the moon" structure is the **visible approaching deadline**, not necessarily timeline-reset gameplay.

Possible escalation:

```text
far out
    strange observations / rumors

mid-term
    dungeons/phenomena become more active

near
    refugees, mobilization, markets/factions reacting

final days
    world broadly understands that something is coming
```

Outcome should not be binary world deletion.

Possible results:

- exceptional preparation: crisis contained;
- partial success: major losses but civilization adapts;
- poor preparation: cities/regions fall and the aftermath becomes the new simulation state;
- ignored: the world simulation resolves what happens without special player protection.

After a campaign-level "win," play continues for decades. The interesting payoff is seeing historical consequences, descendants, rebuilding, changed factions/ecology, and later crises.

Avoid committing yet to plague/war/monster apocalypse/time-splitting as the specific crisis.
