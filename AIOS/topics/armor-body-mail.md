# Body Mail / Hybrid Fitted Armor

**Status:** TENTATIVE

"Body Mail" is a proposed crafted armor form rather than a resource/material.

The visual/mechanical concept is a tightly fitted, body-contoured plate-and-mail construction: closer to a metal second skin than a large rigid breastplate, but not simply a Roman muscle cuirass.

Possible construction:

```text
Chest / Back
    fitted shaped plate

Sides / Waist
    smaller articulated plate sections
    leather-laced/backed

Shoulders / Upper Arms
    overlapping articulated plates

Elbows / Armpits / flex points
    chain + leather

Forearms
    fitted plate

Interior / articulation
    leather, hard leather, padding, lacing
```

Likely recipe families:

```text
Metal
Chain
Hard Leather
Leather
```

The design goal is **high protection with substantially better mobility/flexibility than ordinary full plate**, paid for through higher construction complexity, fit sensitivity, material complexity, and repair difficulty.

It should be sideways progression, not strictly "better plate."

Potential characteristic:

- extremely wearer-specific fit;
- properly fitted Body Mail performs very well;
- wearing someone else's fitted armor may produce larger mobility penalties than ordinary plate.
