# Actor Motivations, Aspirations, and Predispositions

**Status:** PARKED

These ideas are intentionally retained without expanding the Sim Core immediately.

Potential future actor-identity/motivation layer:

- aspirations;
- wants;
- desires;
- "have/need" wants distinct from physiological Needs;
- long-term personal goals;
- predispositions;
- naturally gifted or deficient traits.

Example predisposition:

```text
Gifted Smith
    persistent effective bonus / learning bias

Poor Smithing Aptitude
    persistent effective penalty
    e.g. -25 effective smithing capability
```

The exact semantics are not settled. A predisposition could affect:

- effective Skill;
- learning rate;
- quality ceiling;
- friction/desire toward an activity;
- some combination.

Guardrail:

> Do not add these concepts simply because life-sim games have them. Add them only when they create distinct useful behavior that cannot already be represented cleanly by Attributes, Skills, personality weights, preferences, Needs, or Knowledge.
